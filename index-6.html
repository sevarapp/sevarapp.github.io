A bit about me

I grew up in Samarkand. In high school I took math, CS, and bio all at once and never really picked one, so data science let me keep all three.

Back home I helped run Uzbekistan's IT community and started STEMReach, where 95 girls built their first websites. That one still makes me happy.

These days I also tutor calc and linear algebra, handle the money for the Uzbek Students Organization, and help run the Claude Builder Club.
